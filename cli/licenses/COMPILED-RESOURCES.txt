Compiled renderer release blocker

The packaged nativePreviewWasm.js is executable compiled WebAssembly. Its
preferred-form generating source, complete build scripts, compiler/toolchain
inputs, font-source provenance and licensing for the combination are not present
in this repository. Keep all normal font functionality in this local candidate,
but do not approve public distribution until these materials and rights have
been supplied and reviewed. A resource label or a separately named file does not
create a linking exception. No WebUI-wide AGPL grant is made here.
