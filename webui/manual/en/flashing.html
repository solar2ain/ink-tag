First flashing and wired recovery

Full installs InkTag on factory firmware, updates fixed Recovery or repairs a device that cannot be recovered wirelessly. Ordinary application updates use OTA. Verify the exact MCU, PCB, supply and file checksum first.

Flashing environment

No Keil, Arm compiler or frontend build setup is required. Install separately licensed vendor tools/drivers and loaders, not a firmware compilation environment.

Wiring

SWD targets need supply, common ground, SWDIO, SWCLK and probe voltage reference. VTref is not reliable power output. TLSR uses SWS, not generic SWD. Match the exact PCB, keep contacts short and stable, and never apply 5 V or guess unmarked pads.

EL022H2 wiring does not automatically qualify EL029H2. DA asel29 has TP5=SWCLK, TP6=SWDIO; use actual signal labels for the 4.2-inch large-pad PCB rather than copying TP positions. Do not connect DA VPP/TP3 or program OTP. DA reset is active-high, not ordinary active-low nRESET.

The default Windows entry requires a factory backup. Explicit -SkipStockBackupCheck skips that requirement without requiring a backup file; chip, capacity, layout, voltage and image checks still apply. Keep recoverable factory copies separately whenever possible.

Use flash/windows/flash-goodix.ps1, flash-om6626.ps1 or flash-telink.ps1 with -Board set to the lowercase public MCU/PCB ID, -Firmware for the matching Full and -StockBackup or the explicit skip option. Use -h for exact dependencies and options. Goodix requires GProgrammer; GR551x also uses J-Link identification. OM requires J-Link and the vendor device pack, preserving the fixed state/code/device-information order. Telink requires the Python UART-to-SWS tool and explicit experimental-layout confirmation where applicable. Do not interchange board-specific Full containers.

Invoke Windows launchers with powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\flash\windows\flash-goodix.ps1 -h, replacing the script for OM/TLSR. Commands require the exact matching Full/runtime in your package.

Linux entry points report support and preparation. A Windows EXE or PowerShell launch is not qualified native Linux flashing. Unsupported targets refuse hardware writes; bash flash/linux/flash.sh --help lists the current scope. macOS has no one-command Full support. Nordic and Dialog require their independently qualified targets and packages; check the support table.

Wait for programming and readback verification, follow the power/BOOT instructions, then check the App identity, firmware and panel. For failures, check supply, ground, wiring and tool ownership before a bounded retry. Do not blindly erase unknown regions.

Nordic and Dialog differences

nRF52811 keeps App internally and fonts externally. First installation needs the same-version internal Full and font assets; OTA does not supply missing fonts. A preparation-only writer test is not physical qualification or a complete public entry.

DA Full includes boot, App, Recovery and fonts, unlike Nordic's separate font process. asel29 defaults to SSD1619 128×296 BWR; asel42 to UC8176 400×300 BWR, not SSD1683. Verify the fitted screen after installation.

Probe/backup may halt the MCU; do not use during clock/power observation. A backup may be old InkTag rather than factory firmware, so retain provenance and checksum. Backup skip does not bypass current identity, voltage or required UICR preservation.
