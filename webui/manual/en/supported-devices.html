The earlier and later StellarPro-154R-N share a product name but use different boards: _00 has a replaceable panel and _02 has a fixed panel. Their panel drivers differ. Matching pins do not make their complete board or screen configurations interchangeable.

E0154A07-A1 is not an established interchangeable replacement for the tested P154030-MF1-A / P154030-0306 assembly. Check the product, board marking and FPC separately rather than choosing by 200×200 resolution alone.

The new TLSR firmware with startup signature verification is still being tested and is not yet a recommended installation. Existing display/function tests above do not replace startup and complete-flashing checks for that new version. See tested screens for panel coverage and partial refresh.

The detailed device table includes screen sizes and installation notes. Early GR5515 and some DA14585 boards need further identification before selecting an installation package.

ZKC21V and ZKC42VM have different hardware variants, and Nebular-420R-N can have different screens. Some boards share a marking with and without NFC; select the configuration matching the hardware fitted to your board.

This release includes WebUI, CLI, user guides and firmware for supported devices, including nRF52832, nRF52811 and DA14585. Firmware and complete-flashing tools are provided by controller and board. Use only actual Release attachments. Installation on TLSR boards with unconfirmed screen compatibility is not recommended.

See tested screens and partial refresh, device/screen details, complete flashing, and OTA/Recovery. Hardware capability and the connected device's declarations determine which functions are available.
